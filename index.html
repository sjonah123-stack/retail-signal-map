          What's on the map

          Update store data

          The map ships with a saved snapshot. Refresh any chain from OpenStreetMap (a few minutes), or open All the Places brand files (.geojson) or CSVs with brand, latitude and longitude columns. Save afterward to keep the result.

          How to read it

          Every cell of the map (about 1.7 miles across at a 5-mile radius) scores the weights of the signals with a store within the radius. Shading starts at the threshold you set in Top areas.

          Neighborhood figures combine the census tracts whose centers fall inside the trade area, weighted by households. They're averages of tract medians, a good screen but not a true median.

          The percentage beside each chain in Signals compares the stores found with the chain's latest reported count. Brand-new stores often reach Google Maps first, so use the links in each site report to confirm what's open.

          ZIP figures come from the nearest ZIP code with Zillow data, so near a ZIP boundary they may describe the neighboring ZIP. Apartment pipeline compares units permitted in 5+ unit buildings over three years with the county's existing apartment stock; permits aren't starts, and some never get built.

          Privacy

          No accounts, cookies or analytics. Uploaded deal lists are kept only in this browser's local storage. Searches and deal-list addresses without coordinates go to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim geocoder; map tiles come from Esri. Clearing a deal list removes it from this browser.
